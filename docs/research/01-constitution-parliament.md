# Research Notes — Constitution & Parliament

*Source: research agent run, June 2026. Used to draft chapters 02 (Constitution), 03 (Rights/Duties/DPSP), 06 (Lok Sabha), 07 (Rajya Sabha), 08 (How a Bill Becomes Law). Verified against PRS, Constitution of India text, Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha official sites.*

## Constituent Assembly
- First sitting 9 Dec 1946; 207 members initially, eventual full 389 (292 from British India provinces + 93 from princely states + 4 from chief commissioner provinces); dropped to 299 after Partition.
- 167 days of plenary; "2 years, 11 months, 18 days".
- Key figures: Rajendra Prasad (President), B.R. Ambedkar (Chairman, Drafting Committee), Nehru (Objectives Resolution), Patel (Advisory Committee), B.N. Rau (Constitutional Adviser), Munshi, Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar.
- Adopted 26 Nov 1949 (Constitution Day); commenced 26 Jan 1950 (Republic Day — chosen for the 1930 Lahore Session Purna Swaraj declaration anniversary).

- Ireland: DPSP, presidential election method, RS nominations

## Preamble
- Full text (1949 + 42nd Amdt 1976 additions of "Socialist", "Secular", "integrity")
- Balram Singh v Union of India (2024) — SC upheld 42nd Amdt insertions; consistent with Constitution's existing ideals

## Fundamental Rights (Part III, Arts 12–35)
1. Equality (14–18)
2. Freedom (19–22) — six freedoms; Art 21 (life/liberty); Art 22 (arrest/detention)
3. Against Exploitation (23–24)
4. Religion (25–28)
5. Cultural & Educational (29–30)

- Writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto.

- 11 duties listed

## Basic Structure doctrine
- Kesavananda Bharati (1973) — 7:6 — Parliament can't destroy basic structure
- Minerva Mills (1980) — judicial review & FR-DPSP balance part of basic structure
- I.R. Coelho (2007) — even 9th Schedule laws post-24-Apr-1973 reviewable

## Amendment procedure (Art 368) — three modes
1. Simple majority (e.g., Art 4 — new States; Art 169 — abolish/create LCs; Schedules 5/6)
2. Special majority (most amendments)
3. Special majority + ratification by ≥½ states (federal: President's election, Art 73/162, judiciary, Schedule VII, Parliament representation, Art 368)

## Recent amendments
- 101st (2016) — GST; Art 279A
- 102nd (2018) — NCBC constitutional status (Arts 338B, 342A)
- 103rd (2019) — 10% EWS reservation
- 104th (2020) — SC/ST extension to 25 Jan 2030; ABOLISHED Anglo-Indian nomination

## Parliament
- Art 79: President + Council of States + House of the People = Parliament
- New Sansad Bhavan inaugurated 28 May 2023; first used 19 Sept 2023; old "Sansad Bhavan" → renamed "Samvidhan Sadan" (Lutyens-Baker, 1921–27, opened Jan 1927 as Council House)
- Three sessions: Budget (Jan/Feb-Apr; Budget on 1 Feb), Monsoon (Jul-Aug), Winter (Nov-Dec)
- Art 85: max 6 months gap between sessions
- Art 100: quorum 1/10 of total members
- Art 108: joint sitting (used thrice — Dowry Prohibition 1961, Banking Service Commission Repeal 1978, POTA 2002)

- Current: 543 elected (Anglo-Indian nomination abolished by 104th Amdt, effective 25 Jan 2020)
- SC reservation: 84; ST reservation: 47; General: 412
- Term: 5 years (Art 83); extendable by 1 year during Emergency
- Election: direct, FPTP, universal adult suffrage (Art 326). Voting age 21→18 by 61st Amdt 1988 (effective 28 Mar 1989)

## Rajya Sabha
- Max strength 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated by President — Art 80)
- 12 nominees: literature, science, art, social service (borrowed from Ireland)
- Current: 245 (233 elected + 12 nominated)
- PERMANENT body; 1/3 retire every 2 years; 6-year term
- INDIRECT election: by elected MLAs of state via STV-PR; OPEN ballot since 2003 RPA amendment
- Chair: VP ex officio (Art 64); Deputy Chair elected
- [Full state-wise table — see chapter draft]
- A&N, Chandigarh, DNH+DD, Ladakh, Lakshadweep: NO RS representation
- Exclusive powers:
  - Art 249: 2/3 vote → empower Parliament to legislate on State List subject for ≤1 year (renewable)
  - Art 312: 2/3 vote → create new All-India Service
- Cannot: originate money bill, vote on demands for grants, move no-confidence

## Lawmaking
- Ordinary: 3 readings each house → joint sitting if deadlock → President's assent
- Money Bill (Art 110): only LS introduction (President's recommendation); RS must return within 14 days (recommendations advisory); Speaker certifies finality (Art 110(3)) — Roger Mathew (2019)/Aadhaar litigation has questioned practice
- Financial Bill (Art 117): some money provisions + others; normal procedure; needs Presidential recommendation
- Constitutional Amendment (Art 368): special majority; NO joint sitting available
- President's assent (Art 111): assent / withhold (absolute veto) / return (suspensive — must assent if re-passed; not for money bills) / pocket veto (no time limit). Zail Singh used pocket veto on Indian Post Office (Amdt) Bill 1986.

## Committees
- PAC: 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS); chaired by Opposition MP; reviews CAG reports
- Estimates Committee: 30 LS members only
- COPU: 22 (15 LS + 7 RS); reviews PSUs
- 24 DRSCs: 31 members each (21 LS + 10 RS); 1-year term; created 1993 (originally 17, expanded to 24 in 2004)
- BAC, Rules Committee, Privileges, General Purposes Committee
- Ad-hoc: Select Committees, JPCs

## Parliamentary devices
- Question Hour 11am-12noon; Starred (oral + supplementary), Unstarred (written), Short Notice (<10 days)
- Zero Hour: 12 noon; Indian innovation (1962); not in rules; ≤30 min; notice to Speaker before 10am
- Calling Attention Motion: Speaker Hukam Singh innovation 1954
- Adjournment Motion: LS only; 50 MPs needed; censure device; ≥2.5 hours
- No-confidence: LS only; 50 MPs to admit
- Confidence Motion
- Cut Motions: Policy Cut (to ₹1), Economy Cut (specified amount), Token Cut (₹100). If passed = no-confidence
