# Research Notes — Executive, Judiciary & Constitutional Bodies

*Source: research agent run, June 2026. Used to draft chapters 05 (Union Executive), 09 (State Government), 10 (Judiciary), 11 (ECI & Constitutional Bodies).*

## President of India (Arts 52–62)
- Constitutional head; Art 53 vests executive power
- Eligibility: citizen, 35+, qualified for LS, no office of profit
- Electoral College: elected MPs (both houses) + elected MLAs of 28 states + elected MLAs of Delhi, Puducherry, J&K. Nominated MPs/MLAs and MLCs do NOT vote.
- System: STV-PR, secret ballot (Art 55(3))
- Vote-value formulas (uses 1971 census per 42nd Amdt, extended by 84th Amdt to "first census after 2026"):
  - MLA's vote = (State's 1971 population / elected MLAs) / 1000
  - MP's vote = (total MLA vote value) / total elected MPs
- Worked examples: UP MLA ≈ 208; Sikkim MLA = 7; Delhi MLA = 58
- 2022 election: Murmu 2,161 votes worth 5,77,777; Sinha 1,058 worth 2,61,062; total EC value 10,86,431
- Term 5 yrs; re-elect; oath by CJI (Art 60)
- Powers: executive (appointments), legislative (assent, summon/prorogue, dissolve LS, ordinance Art 123), judicial (pardon Art 72), financial (Money Bill rec), diplomatic, military (Supreme Commander), emergency (Art 352/356/360)
- Impeachment (Art 61): only ground = "violation of Constitution"; 1/4 of total membership signatures + 14-day notice; 2/3 of total membership in initiating House; other house investigates and must pass by 2/3 of total membership; nominated MPs vote in impeachment; no Indian President ever impeached
- Presidents list: Rajendra Prasad (1950–62; only 2 terms), Radhakrishnan, Zakir Husain, V.V. Giri, Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed, N. Sanjiva Reddy (unopposed), Zail Singh, Venkataraman, Shankar Dayal Sharma, K.R. Narayanan (first Dalit), Kalam, Pratibha Patil (first woman), Pranab Mukherjee, Kovind, **Droupadi Murmu (15th, 25 Jul 2022 →)** — first ST President, second woman, first born after independence

## Vice President (Arts 63–73)
- Ex officio Chair of Rajya Sabha (Art 64); discharges President's functions during vacancy (Art 65)
- EC: ALL MPs (elected + nominated) — STV/PR secret ballot. Only MPs; no state legislators.
- Removal: Art 67(b) — RS resolution by majority of all then members + LS simple majority; 14-day notice. ONLY constitutional functionary removable by effective majority.
- **Jagdeep Dhankhar** sworn in 11 Aug 2022; **resigned 21 Jul 2025** citing health — third early VP vacancy.
- Election held 9 Sept 2025. **C.P. Radhakrishnan** (NDA) beat B. Sudarshan Reddy (INDIA), 452 EC votes; sworn in 12 Sept 2025 as 15th VP.

## PM & Council of Ministers (Arts 74, 75)
- Art 74(1): CoM with PM at head to aid & advise President; President shall act on advice
- Modi sworn in for 3rd consecutive term 9 Jun 2024 (only Nehru did same before)
- CoM: 30 Cabinet + 5 MoS-IC + 36 MoS = 72 (within 15% cap of 543 LS) [some figures need cross-verification]
- Categories: Cabinet, MoS-IC, MoS (no constitutional distinction; Cabinet is subset of CoM)
- 91st Amdt (2003): Art 75(1A) caps CoM at 15% of LS strength (notified 7 Jan 2004); parallel Art 164(1A) for states (min 12); also blocks disqualified members from being ministers
- Collective responsibility (Art 75(3)) to LS
- Cabinet Committees (Modi 3.0 — 8 committees re-constituted 3 Jul 2024; PM chairs 6):
  - CCS (Security): PM, HM, FM, DM, EAM
  - CCEA, CCPA (Political), CCPA (Parliamentary), ACC (Appointments), CCI&G, CCE&SD, CCAcc
- PMO: Principal Secretary = P.K. Mishra (Cabinet rank, 1972 IAS Gujarat); NSA = Ajit Doval (1968 IPS, former IB Director)
- AG: R. Venkataramani; reappointed 1 Oct 2025 for 2-yr term → 30 Sept 2027

## Governor (Arts 153–162)
- Appointed by President (Art 155); holds during President's pleasure (Art 156(1)); 5 yrs normal term; citizen, 35+
- Convention: not from the State
- Discretionary powers: CM appt in hung assembly, dissolve, Art 356 recommendation, Art 200 reserve bills, Art 167 info
- B.P. Singhal v UoI (2010, Constitution Bench): pleasure NOT absolute; judicially reviewable for malafides/arbitrariness; cannot be removed because "out of sync" or "change of govt"
- State of Punjab v Principal Secretary to Governor (Nov 2023, CJI DY Chandrachud + Pardiwala + Misra): Governor cannot doubt validity of Assembly session, cannot sit on bills; symbolic head, cannot thwart lawmaking
- State of Tamil Nadu v Governor of TN (8 Apr 2025, 2025 INSC 481, Pardiwala + Mahadevan): Governor R.N. Ravi's withholding of 10 bills (some since Jan 2020) illegal; Art 200 — no absolute/pocket veto; must act on Council advice; once re-passed, must assent. SC fixed timelines (1 month with advice; 3 months without; 1 month for re-passed). Invoked Art 142 to "deem" 10 state bills as assented — first time in Indian constitutional history.

## State CM & CoM (Arts 163, 164)
- CM appointed by Governor; ministers on CM advice
- Art 164(1A): cap 15% of Assembly, min 12

## UTs
- After 31 Oct 2019 (J&K Reorg Act 2019): J&K (with Assembly) + Ladakh (without Assembly)
- 26 Jan 2020: DNH + DD merged → 1 UT
- Total: 8 UTs — A&N, Chandigarh, DNH+DD, Delhi, J&K, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Puducherry
- With Assemblies: Delhi, Puducherry, J&K
- Delhi: Art 239AA + GNCTD Act 1991
  - GNCTD (Amdt) Act 2021: enhanced LG role
  - 2023 SC Constitution Bench (11 May 2023): elected Delhi govt has services power (Entry 41 List II), except public order/police/land
  - GNCTD (Amdt) Act 2023 (11 Aug 2023): created NCCSA (3-member: CM as chair + Chief Secretary + Principal Home Secretary); LG has final say. Overrode SC ruling.
- LG appointed under Art 239

## Supreme Court (Arts 124–147)
- Original: 7 judges + CJI (1950). Strength revised 6 times.
- SC (Number of Judges) Amdt Act 2019: max 34 (CJI + 33)
- CJIs recently: Khanna (51st, sworn 11 Nov 2024 → May 2025); Gavai (52nd, sworn 14 May 2025 → 23 Nov 2025); **Surya Kant (53rd, sworn 24 Nov 2025 → 9 Feb 2027)**
- Collegium: CJI + 4 senior-most SC judges (Second Judges 1993, Third Judges 1998)
- NJAC (99th Amdt 2014) struck down 2015 (4th Judges Case, Khehar+Lokur+Goel+Kurian; Chelameswar dissent): judicial primacy = basic structure
- Jurisdiction: original (Art 131 Centre-State, Art 32 writ for FR), appellate (Arts 132–134), SLP Art 136, advisory Art 143, review Art 137, curative (Rupa Ashok Hurra 2002)
- PIL: Krishna Iyer (Mumbai Kamgar Sabha 1976), Bhagwati (SP Gupta v UoI 1981 — First Judges Case)
- Removal: Art 124(4) + Judges (Inquiry) Act 1968. Ground: "proved misbehaviour or incapacity". 100 LS or 50 RS members sign. 3-member committee (SC judge, HC CJ, distinguished jurist). Both Houses, same session, special majority. President issues order. No SC judge ever removed. Closest: Justice V. Ramaswami (10 May 1993, 196 for, 0 against, 205 Congress abstentions — failed).

## High Courts (Arts 214–231)
- 25 HCs (after AP HC bifurcation 1 Jan 2019: AP at Amaravati, Telangana at Hyderabad)
- Multi-state/UT HCs: Bombay (MH, Goa, DNH+DD); Calcutta (WB, A&N); Gauhati (Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram, Arunachal); Madras (TN, Puducherry); Punjab & Haryana (Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh); J&K and Ladakh common HC.
- Appointment Art 217 by President on Collegium (CJI + 2 senior-most SC + HC Collegium of CJ + 2 senior-most HC judges for HC CJ)
- Writ jurisdiction Art 226 — wider than Art 32 (for FRs AND any other purpose)
- Sanctioned strengths vary (Allahabad ~160; Sikkim ~3)

## Subordinate Judiciary (Arts 233–237)
- District judges: Governor in consultation with HC (Art 233)
- Other judicial officers: Art 234; HC controls postings, promotions, discipline (Art 235)

## Tribunals (Arts 323A, 323B — added 42nd Amdt 1976)
- 323A: Parliament only — admin tribunals for service matters. CAT (1985).
- 323B: Parliament + states — taxation, FX, labour, land reforms, elections, rent, food. ITAT, NCLT, NGT, AFT, CESTAT.
- L. Chandra Kumar v UoI (1997): judicial review by HCs (Art 226) and SC (Art 32) = basic structure; tribunals subject to writ scrutiny
- Tribunals Reforms Act 2021: merged several appellate tribunals; standardized tenure

## ECI (Art 324)
- Plenary powers per Mohinder Singh Gill v CEC (1978)
- Originally single-member. Multi-member 16 Oct 1989 (Rajiv Gandhi). Reverted to single 1 Jan 1990 (VP Singh). Permanent multi-member 1 Oct 1993.
- CEC removal: SC judge process; ECs only on CEC recommendation
- Anoop Baranwal v UoI (2 Mar 2023, 5-judge bench, J. K.M. Joseph): until Parliament law, appointment by President on advice of PM + LoP(LS) + CJI
- CEC and Other ECs Act 2023 (notified 28 Dec 2023): PM + LoP(LS) + Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM; Search Committee headed by Cabinet Secretary shortlists 5; tenure 6 yrs or 65; SC judge status. Provision that committee's recommendation valid even when LoP seat vacant — criticized.
- Current (Jun 2026): CEC Gyanesh Kumar (26th, 1988 IAS Kerala, sworn 19 Feb 2025, term till 26 Jan 2029); EC Sukhbir Singh Sandhu; EC Dr. Vivek Joshi (1989 IAS Haryana, sworn 19 Feb 2025)
- Field: CEO (state) → DEO (district) → ERO (AC) → RO/Presiding Officer

## UPSC (Arts 315–323)
- Central recruiting agency for AIS and Central Civil Services
- Chair + members by President (Art 316). Tenure: 6 yrs or 65.
- Removal Art 317: President, after SC reference + report. Direct removal for insolvency/paid employment outside/infirmity.
- ≥1/2 members must have ≥10 yrs central or state govt service

## State PSCs (Art 315)
- Chair + members by Governor; removal by President on SC reference. Age cap 62. 6 yrs term.

## Finance Commission (Art 280)
- Quasi-judicial; every 5 yrs by President
- Recommends: vertical devolution of taxes, horizontal allocation, Art 275 grants, augment state CFs for local bodies, other matters
- 15th FC (N.K. Singh, 2021–26): 41% vertical devolution (down from 14th's 42% after J&K/Ladakh became UTs)
- 16th FC (Arvind Panagariya, constituted 31 Dec 2023; members: T. Rabi Sankar, Annie George Mathew, Manoj Panda, Soumya Kanti Ghosh; period 1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2031): report tabled 1 Feb 2026; recommended retaining 41% share

## CAG (Art 148)
- "Guardian of public purse" (Ambedkar)
- Appointed by President; tenure 6 yrs or 65. Removal: SC judge process.
- Salary charged on CFI (cannot be varied to disadvantage)
- Current (Jun 2026): K. Sanjay Murthy (1989 IAS HP, sworn 21 Nov 2024)

## Other Constitutional Commissions
- NCSC (Art 338) — Chair + VC + 3 Members; 3 yrs; max 2 terms
- NCST (Art 338A) — bifurcated by 89th Amdt 2003
- NCBC (Art 338B) — granted constitutional status by 102nd Amdt 2018; Art 342A — President specifies SEBCs on Governor's consultation
- Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (Art 350B) — added 7th Amdt 1956; HQ Allahabad; offices Belgaum, Chennai, Kolkata
- AG (Art 76); State AG (Art 165) — Governor appoints; HC judge qualifications; holds during Governor's pleasure
