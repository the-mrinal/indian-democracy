## Why this guide exists

You wanted to understand India's government end-to-end: what the Constitution actually says, how Parliament works, how the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha differ, how the President, the Prime Minister, the MLAs, the Governors are picked, what the electoral math really is, and — crucially — how a campaign is actually executed on the ground, booth by booth.

This guide is built in **two layers**:

1. **The System Architecture** — what M. Laxmikanth's *Indian Polity* teaches: the constitutional structure, the offices, the rules.
2. **The Ground-Level Execution** — what Shivam Shankar Singh's *How to Win an Indian Election* teaches: how parties actually identify, persuade, and turn out voters.

Every non-trivial fact is sourced. Primary references are listed at the bottom of each chapter and consolidated in the final **References** chapter.

## A note on citations

Web sources are linked inline in each chapter and consolidated in a **Sources** block at the bottom. Where a number is time-sensitive (e.g., seat counts after delimitation, current officeholders), the date of the figure is stated. Where a fact could not be independently verified at build time it is marked `[unverified]`.
